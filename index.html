Spike Flatteners — before your first meal

  • Water first on waking.Rehydrate before anything else.
  • Psyllium drink — 1 tsp in a glass of water, ~10 min before eating.Forms the gel that slows sugar in — like the funnel. Then chase with LOTS more water or it sits like a cinder block.

Stop feeding it — for 3 weeks

  • No alcohol STOPA metabolic brake — your liver drops everything to process it and fat-burning stalls.
  • No ultra-processed food STOPNo fiber, no work for your gut — sugar floods in, insulin surges, the switch slams on. Eat whole foods with the same calories and 3× the volume.

Walk — the exercise nobody respects

  • 10,000–15,000 steps a dayBreak it up all day. 15k ≈ 500 cal → a pound of fat a week from walking alone. A walking pad under your desk is the best cheap investment.
  • Walking HIIT — 2–3×/week5 min warm-up → 4 rounds of [90s fast / 90s slow] → cool down. ~25 min total.

Lift — muscle is the switch

Muscle is a sponge: it soaks up carbs as glycogen instead of letting them flood your blood and store as fat. More muscle = more sensitive = less storage, higher metabolism around the clock.

Resistance train 3×/week minimum. You don't need to become a bodybuilder — just show up.

Fasting — 6 days a week

  • 16:8 dailyEat inside an 8-hour window (e.g. noon → 8pm), fast 16. Eight of those hours you're asleep.
  • One 24-hour fast per weekLast meal one night 8pm → next dinner 8pm. Drops insulin to the floor, triggers autophagy, boosts growth hormone, gives your gut a full day to reset.

Fasting is a tool, not a religion — more isn't better. Real visceral fat loss compounds after the 8–12 week mark; these 3 weeks build the habit and the strong start.

Cortisol — the hidden switch

Do everything right and still stuck? Chronic cortisol makes your body hoard fat around your organs as a survival response. It's anti-testosterone: stores fat, burns muscle, drains you. Driven not just by work — but by under-eating, over-exercising, poor sleep, and scrolling the second you wake.
